# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Workers in informal trades use KaamPass to show their skills and keep a portable work history. Employers use it to find workers, agree on work and confirm completed jobs. A public visitor can view a worker's shared profile.

## Product Purpose

Help workers build a portable record of jobs confirmed by both the worker and employer. Verification means mutual confirmation; it does not independently verify identity, skills or payment.

## Operating Context

The MVP supports electricians, plumbers and painters. Employers can post requests or offer work directly. Workers can bid, review agreements and confirm completed work. Users need a fast, understandable interface on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep existing authentication, profiles, portfolio, public sharing, jobs, agreements and feedback. Private earnings and job details remain private. Support English, Roman Urdu and Urdu, including right-to-left layout.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the KaamPass name and existing logo. The user has approved refreshing the colours. Use short, clear language.

## Evidence on Hand

Product details and fictional demonstration accounts are documented in `docs/idea.md` and `README.md`. Do not present demo data as real customer evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make common tasks easy to find and complete.
- Explain what is public before users share it.
- Distinguish confirmed work from unverified claims.
- Preserve accessibility and the three supported languages.
